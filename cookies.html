2. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device when you visit it. They let the site remember things about your visit, such as your preferences or whether you've been here before. Similar technologies, such as pixels, local storage and scripts that read information from your device, work in a similar way. In this policy we call them all "cookies".

First-party cookies are set by us. Third-party cookies are set by another company's service that we use on our site. Some cookies last only until you close your browser (session cookies); others stay on your device for a set period (persistent cookies).

3. How we use cookies

We group the cookies on our website into two categories:

  • Strictly necessary cookies are needed for the website to work securely, for example to protect our forms from spam and abuse and to remember your cookie choices. The law allows us to use these without asking for your consent.
  • Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors find and use the website, which pages are popular and how people move between them, so we can improve it. They also help us connect a waitlist or contact form submission with earlier visits. We only set these cookies if you agree to them.

We do not use advertising or targeting cookies, and we do not sell any information collected through cookies.

2. Our role: controller or processor

Under data protection law, the organisation that decides why and how personal data is used is the controller. An organisation that handles personal data on a controller's instructions is a processor.

  • We are the controller for personal data about website visitors, waitlist members, people who contact us, and the staff and representatives of our customers, prospects and suppliers. This policy explains how we handle that data.
  • We are usually a processor for personal data about the individuals and businesses our customers verify through the Verifsage platform ("End Users"). In that case the customer, such as the bank, fintech or marketplace you are dealing with, is the controller and decides why your information is checked. Their own privacy notice explains how they use it, and our Data Processing Agreement governs what we may do with it. Section 6 gives an outline.

6. If you are verified through Verifsage

If a business asks you to verify your identity or your company using Verifsage, that business is the controller of your information and decides what checks are carried out. Depending on the checks it chooses, we may process on its behalf:

  • identity information such as your name, date of birth, address and nationality;
  • images of identity documents and the information they contain;
  • a selfie or short video used for face matching and liveness checks. Facial data used to confirm your identity is biometric data, a special category of personal data that receives extra protection by law;
  • results of sanctions, politically exposed person (PEP) and adverse-media screening, which can include information about alleged or proven offences;
  • information about companies, their directors and beneficial owners; and
  • device and technical information used to detect fraud.

We use this information only to perform the checks the business has asked for, to keep the platform secure and to meet our own legal obligations. We do not use it for our own marketing. The business, not Verifsage, decides whether to accept you as a customer. If you have questions about a verification, or want to exercise your rights, please contact the business that asked you to verify; we will help them respond. 8. International transfers

Some of our service providers process personal data outside the United Kingdom. When that happens we make sure the transfer is protected as UK data protection law requires, by relying on:

  • UK adequacy regulations, which recognise that a country gives an adequate level of protection, including the EEA;
  • for US providers, their certification under the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, where they hold one; or
  • the ICO's International Data Transfer Agreement, or the ICO's Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, together with any additional safeguards needed.

Customers can choose UK, EU or US hosting for verification data, as described in our Data Processing Agreement. You can contact us for more information about the safeguards we use.

10. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • be told how your personal data is used, which is the purpose of this policy;
  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your data;
  • ask us to restrict how we use your data;
  • object to our use of your data where we rely on legitimate interests, and to stop direct marketing at any time;
  • ask us to transfer data you gave us to you or another organisation, in a commonly used format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent. This does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it; and
  • not be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing that has legal or similarly significant effects on you, with some exceptions.

To use any of these rights, email privacy@verifsage.com. We will respond within one month, and may ask you to confirm your identity first. There is normally no fee. If you were verified through Verifsage on behalf of another business, see section 6.

11. Automated decision-making

Verifsage does not make decisions about website visitors, waitlist members or business contacts using solely automated processing.

Our platform produces automated verification and screening results for our customers. Our customers decide how to use those results, and they are responsible for any human review, explanation or right to challenge that the law requires before a decision is made about you. If a decision has been made about you following a verification, please contact the business concerned.

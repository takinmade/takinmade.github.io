Privacy policy

Last updated: 22nd November 2026

Beyond The Details plans weddings, engagements, private celebrations and brand events across the UK. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you contact us or work with us, why we need it, how we look after it and the rights you have over it.

Who we are

Beyond The Details is the data controller for the personal information described here. Beyond The Details is run by [legal name of owner or company, and company number if limited], [business address].

For any question about this policy or your information, email beyondthedetailseventsuk@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry through our website we collect:

  • your name, and your partner's name if you give it
  • your email address and phone number
  • the service you're interested in, your estimated budget and guest numbers
  • your event date and venue, if known
  • how you heard about us
  • anything you choose to tell us in your message

If you go on to book with us, we will also hold the information needed to plan and run your event. This can include details of your guests (for example names, dietary requirements and accessibility needs), supplier and venue arrangements, and payment and invoicing records.

If you give us information about other people, such as your partner or your guests, please make sure they are happy for you to share it with us.

How we use it and why we're allowed to

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a proposal. This is a step you've asked us to take before entering into a contract.
  • To plan and deliver your event, including sharing the relevant details with your venue and suppliers. This is necessary to perform our contract with you.
  • To keep financial records such as invoices and payments. This is a legal obligation.
  • To manage guest dietary or accessibility needs you tell us about. We only use this to look after your guests on the day, share it only with the caterer or venue who needs it, and delete it after the event.
  • To ask for a review or share photos of your event. We will only publish your name, review or photos of you with your permission, and you can withdraw that permission at any time.

We will never sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Who we share it with

  • venues and suppliers working on your event, only what they need
  • the service providers that run our business, such as our email provider (Google), our website host GoDaddy and our enquiry form provider web3forms
  • our accountant, and authorities such as HMRC where the law requires it

Some of these providers may store data outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on the safeguards UK law requires, such as UK adequacy regulations or the International Data Transfer Agreement.

How long we keep it

  • enquiries that don't lead to a booking: up to 12 months, then deleted
  • client records and invoices: 6 years after your event, to meet tax and legal requirements
  • guest dietary and accessibility details: deleted after the event

Cookies and this website

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. To display our fonts it loads them from Google Fonts, which means your browser shares your IP address with Google when the page loads.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct anything that's wrong
  • ask us to delete your information
  • ask us to limit how we use it, or object to how we use it
  • ask us to send it to you or someone else in a usable format
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on it

To use any of these rights, email beyondthedetailseventsuk@gmail.com. We'll respond within one month.

Keeping it safe

We keep your information in password-protected accounts with two-step verification turned on, and only share it with people who need it to work on your event.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated at the top.

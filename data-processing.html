Parties and scope

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the agreement, Terms of Service, Order Form or other written or electronic agreement governing the customer's use of the Verifsage services (the "Agreement") between:

(1) [[VERIFSAGE LEGAL ENTITY NAME]], a company incorporated in [[JURISDICTION]] under company number [[NUMBER]], whose registered office is at [[REGISTERED ADDRESS]] ("Verifsage", "Processor", "we", "us" or "our"); and

(2) the customer identified in the Agreement ("Customer" or "Controller").

This DPA applies where Verifsage Processes Personal Data on behalf of Customer in connection with the Services. By entering into the Agreement or using the Services, each party agrees to this DPA.

Where Customer acts as a Processor on behalf of another Controller, references in this DPA to Customer as Controller shall include Customer acting as Processor and Verifsage shall act as Customer's Subprocessor to the extent applicable.

1. Definitions

1.1In this DPA:

"Applicable Data Protection Law" means all laws and regulations applicable to the Processing of Personal Data under this DPA, including, as applicable:

(a) Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("EU GDPR");

(b) the EU GDPR as incorporated into the law of the United Kingdom and amended from time to time ("UK GDPR");

(c) the Data Protection Act 2018;

(d) the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 to the extent applicable;

(e) applicable national legislation implementing, supplementing or replacing the foregoing; and

(f) binding regulatory requirements relating to privacy, data protection or the Processing of Personal Data.

"Controller", "Data Subject", "Personal Data", "Personal Data Breach", "Processing", "Processor" and "Supervisory Authority" have the meanings given to them under Applicable Data Protection Law.

"Customer Personal Data" means Personal Data Processed by Verifsage on behalf of Customer in connection with the Services.

"EU SCCs" means the standard contractual clauses annexed to Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 of 4 June 2021, as amended, replaced or superseded.

"Restricted Transfer" means a transfer of Personal Data that requires an adequacy mechanism, appropriate safeguard or other lawful transfer mechanism under Applicable Data Protection Law.

"Services" means the products and services supplied by Verifsage under the Agreement.

"Subprocessor" means a third party appointed by or on behalf of Verifsage to Process Customer Personal Data.

"UK Addendum" means the International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Commission Standard Contractual Clauses issued by the UK Information Commissioner's Office, as amended, replaced or superseded.

1.2Capitalised terms not defined in this DPA have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

2. Scope and roles

2.1Customer is the Controller and Verifsage is the Processor in respect of Customer Personal Data, except where Customer acts as Processor for another Controller, in which case Verifsage is a Subprocessor.

2.2Each party shall comply with the obligations applicable to it under Applicable Data Protection Law.

2.3Customer is responsible for determining the purposes and lawful basis of the Processing, providing any notices required to Data Subjects, obtaining any required consents, and ensuring that its instructions to Verifsage comply with Applicable Data Protection Law.

2.4Verifsage shall Process Customer Personal Data only:

(a) to provide, secure, maintain and support the Services;

(b) in accordance with the Agreement, this DPA and Customer's documented instructions; or

(c) where required by applicable law.

2.5If applicable law requires Verifsage to Process Customer Personal Data other than on Customer's documented instructions, Verifsage shall inform Customer before carrying out that Processing unless the applicable law prohibits such disclosure.

2.6Verifsage shall promptly inform Customer if, in Verifsage's reasonable opinion, an instruction infringes Applicable Data Protection Law. Verifsage may suspend the affected Processing until the parties resolve the issue.

3. Details of Processing

3.1The subject matter, duration, nature and purpose of the Processing, categories of Personal Data and categories of Data Subjects are set out in Schedule One.

3.2Processing will continue for the duration of the Agreement and for such additional period as is necessary to return or delete Customer Personal Data in accordance with this DPA or to comply with applicable law.

4. Confidentiality and personnel

4.1Verifsage shall ensure that persons authorised to Process Customer Personal Data:

(a) have access only where reasonably necessary to perform their responsibilities;

(b) are subject to appropriate contractual or statutory confidentiality obligations; and

(c) receive appropriate privacy and information-security awareness or training relevant to their roles.

4.2Verifsage shall take reasonable steps to ensure the reliability of personnel with access to Customer Personal Data.

5. Security

5.1Taking into account the state of the art, implementation costs, and the nature, scope, context and purposes of Processing together with the risk to Data Subjects, Verifsage shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect Customer Personal Data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of or access to Customer Personal Data.

5.2The measures maintained by Verifsage shall include the measures described in Schedule Two.

5.3Verifsage may update its technical and organisational measures from time to time provided that such changes do not materially reduce the overall level of protection afforded to Customer Personal Data during the term of the Agreement.

5.4Customer acknowledges that security involves responsibilities on both sides. Customer shall use the Services in accordance with Verifsage's security documentation, protect account credentials, maintain appropriate access controls for its users and promptly notify Verifsage of suspected compromise affecting the Services.

6. Subprocessors

6.1Customer grants Verifsage general written authorisation to appoint Subprocessors for the Processing of Customer Personal Data.

6.2Verifsage shall maintain a current list of its Subprocessors at [[SUBPROCESSOR URL]] or in Schedule Three.

6.3Verifsage shall provide Customer with at least 30 days' prior notice, where reasonably practicable, before appointing a new Subprocessor that will materially Process Customer Personal Data or replacing an existing such Subprocessor. Notice may be provided by email, through the Services or through a subscription mechanism made available on Verifsage's website.

6.4Customer may object to a proposed Subprocessor within 15 days of receiving notice, but only on reasonable and documented data-protection grounds.

6.5The parties shall work in good faith to resolve an objection. Where no commercially reasonable alternative is available, Verifsage may discontinue the affected part of the Services and Customer may terminate that affected part without penalty.

6.6Verifsage shall enter into a written agreement with each Subprocessor that imposes data-protection obligations no less protective in substance than those applicable to Verifsage under this DPA, to the extent relevant to the Subprocessor's Processing.

6.7Verifsage remains responsible to Customer for the performance of each Subprocessor's data-protection obligations to the extent required by Applicable Data Protection Law.

7. Data Subject requests

7.1Taking into account the nature of the Processing, Verifsage shall provide reasonable assistance to Customer through appropriate technical and organisational measures to enable Customer to respond to requests from Data Subjects exercising rights under Applicable Data Protection Law.

7.2Where Verifsage receives a Data Subject request relating to Customer Personal Data, Verifsage shall, unless legally prohibited:

(a) notify Customer without undue delay; and

(b) not respond substantively to the request except on Customer's documented instruction or where required by applicable law.

7.3Customer remains responsible for determining whether and how a Data Subject request must be fulfilled.

8. Compliance assistance

8.1Taking into account the nature of Processing and information available to Verifsage, Verifsage shall provide reasonable assistance to Customer in complying with applicable obligations concerning:

(a) security of Processing;

(b) Personal Data Breaches;

(c) data protection impact assessments;

(d) prior consultation with Supervisory Authorities; and

(e) other Processor-assistance obligations imposed by Applicable Data Protection Law.

8.2Verifsage may charge reasonable fees for assistance under this clause where the assistance is unusually extensive and is not required because of Verifsage's breach of this DPA. Verifsage shall notify Customer of applicable charges in advance.

9. Personal Data Breaches

9.1Verifsage shall notify Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Personal Data and shall use reasonable endeavours to provide such notification within 48 hours of awareness.

9.2To the extent information is available, the notification shall describe:

(a) the nature of the Personal Data Breach;

(b) the categories and approximate number of affected Data Subjects;

(c) the categories and approximate volume of affected Personal Data records;

(d) the likely consequences of the Personal Data Breach;

(e) measures taken or proposed to contain, investigate, remediate or mitigate its effects; and

(f) a contact point from which Customer may obtain further information.

9.3Where all information is not available at the time of initial notification, Verifsage may provide information in phases without undue further delay.

9.4Verifsage shall take reasonable steps to contain, investigate and mitigate the Personal Data Breach and shall reasonably cooperate with Customer in relation to Customer's notification, documentation and remediation obligations.

9.5Verifsage's notification of a Personal Data Breach shall not constitute an admission of fault or liability.

9.6Customer is responsible for determining whether notification to a Supervisory Authority, Data Subjects or any other person is required, except to the extent Applicable Data Protection Law places such responsibility directly on Verifsage.

10. Audits and information rights

10.1Verifsage shall make available to Customer information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with the Processor obligations applicable under this DPA and Applicable Data Protection Law.

10.2Customer shall ordinarily exercise its verification rights first through reasonable documentation requests, security questionnaires, certifications or independent assurance reports that Verifsage makes available.

10.3Where that information is insufficient to demonstrate material compliance, Customer may conduct an audit, itself or through an independent auditor, subject to the following conditions:

(a) reasonable prior written notice shall be given;

(b) audits shall ordinarily occur no more than once in any 12-month period unless a Personal Data Breach, reasonable evidence of material non-compliance or a Supervisory Authority requires otherwise;

(c) the audit shall be conducted during normal business hours and in a manner that minimises disruption;

(d) the auditor shall not be a direct competitor of Verifsage and shall be subject to confidentiality obligations;

(e) the audit shall not compromise the security, confidentiality or Personal Data of Verifsage or other customers; and

(f) Customer shall bear its audit costs unless the audit establishes a material breach of this DPA by Verifsage, in which case Verifsage shall bear its reasonable costs of remediation and the parties shall allocate reasonable audit costs as required by law or agreement.

10.4Nothing in this clause limits the powers of a competent Supervisory Authority.

11. International transfers

11.1Verifsage shall not make a Restricted Transfer of Customer Personal Data unless the transfer is supported by a lawful transfer mechanism under Applicable Data Protection Law.

11.2Where an applicable adequacy decision, adequacy regulation or equivalent lawful mechanism covers the Restricted Transfer, the parties may rely on that mechanism.

11.3EEA transfers. Where Customer Personal Data subject to the EU GDPR is transferred to Verifsage or a Subprocessor in a third country that is not covered by an applicable European Commission adequacy decision, the EU SCCs are incorporated into this DPA as follows:

(a) where Customer is Controller and Verifsage is Processor, Module Two applies;

(b) where Customer is Processor and Verifsage is Subprocessor, Module Three applies;

(c) Clause 7, the docking clause, applies;

(d) for Clause 9, Option 2, general written authorisation, applies and the notice period is the period specified in clause 6.3 of this DPA;

(e) the optional language in Clause 11(a) shall not apply unless expressly agreed otherwise;

(f) for Clause 17, the governing law shall be the law of Ireland, unless another eligible EU Member State law is identified in the applicable Order Form or Agreement;

(g) for Clause 18, disputes shall be resolved before the courts of Ireland, unless another eligible EU Member State is identified in the applicable Order Form or Agreement;

(h) Annex I of the EU SCCs shall be deemed completed using the information in the Agreement and Schedule One of this DPA;

(i) Annex II shall be deemed completed by Schedule Two; and

(j) Annex III shall be deemed completed by Schedule Three.

11.4UK transfers. Where a Restricted Transfer is subject to the UK GDPR and is not covered by applicable UK adequacy regulations:

(a) where the EU SCCs apply or are otherwise used by the parties, the UK Addendum is incorporated into this DPA;

(b) the information required by the UK Addendum tables shall be populated using the Agreement, this DPA and Schedules One to Three;

(c) the parties shall be treated as the exporter and importer identified in the applicable EU SCC module;

(d) unless otherwise stated in an Order Form, neither party may terminate the UK Addendum merely as a consequence of an approved revision where the approved Addendum permits parties to specify termination rights; and

(e) where the UK Addendum is unsuitable for the relevant transfer, the parties may enter into the ICO's then-current International Data Transfer Agreement or another lawful transfer mechanism.

11.5Each party shall reasonably cooperate in completing any transfer impact assessment, transfer risk assessment or similar assessment required by Applicable Data Protection Law.

11.6Where required, Verifsage shall implement supplementary technical, contractual or organisational safeguards reasonably necessary to maintain the required level of protection.

11.7If a transfer mechanism relied upon by the parties is invalidated, withdrawn or becomes unavailable, the parties shall cooperate in good faith to implement a lawful alternative.

11.8In the event of conflict between this DPA and the EU SCCs or UK Addendum concerning a Restricted Transfer, the EU SCCs or UK Addendum, as applicable, shall prevail to the extent of the conflict.

12. Government and third-party access requests

12.1Unless prohibited by applicable law, Verifsage shall notify Customer of a legally binding request from a public authority requiring disclosure of Customer Personal Data where Applicable Data Protection Law requires or permits such notification.

12.2Verifsage shall assess the legality of any such request and, where there are reasonable grounds to do so and Applicable Data Protection Law requires it, challenge requests that Verifsage reasonably considers unlawful.

12.3Verifsage shall disclose only the Customer Personal Data reasonably necessary to comply with a legally binding request.

13. Return and deletion

13.1On expiry or termination of the Services involving Processing of Customer Personal Data, and at Customer's choice where technically practicable, Verifsage shall return or delete Customer Personal Data unless applicable law requires continued storage.

13.2Customer shall exercise any available export functionality before termination where it wishes to retain a copy of Customer Personal Data.

13.3Following termination, Verifsage shall delete remaining Customer Personal Data from active systems within [[30/60/90]] days, subject to technical limitations and lawful retention requirements.

13.4Customer Personal Data contained in backups may be retained until overwritten or deleted in accordance with Verifsage's ordinary backup lifecycle, provided that such Personal Data remains protected under this DPA and is not restored or otherwise Processed except for disaster recovery, security or legal purposes.

13.5Where applicable law requires Verifsage to retain particular Customer Personal Data, Verifsage shall isolate and protect that information and Process it only for the purpose requiring retention.

14. Records and regulatory cooperation

14.1Verifsage shall maintain records of Processing activities to the extent required by Applicable Data Protection Law.

14.2Verifsage shall reasonably cooperate with competent Supervisory Authorities in the performance of their lawful functions relating to Processing under this DPA.

15. Liability and indemnities

15.1Nothing in this DPA excludes or limits either party's liability to the extent that liability cannot lawfully be excluded or limited, nor does this DPA restrict the rights of Data Subjects or powers of Supervisory Authorities under Applicable Data Protection Law.

15.2Subject to clause 15.1 and any mandatory liability provisions in the EU SCCs, UK Addendum or Applicable Data Protection Law, each party's liability arising from this DPA shall be subject to the exclusions and limitations of liability in the Agreement.

15.3If the Agreement contains no applicable limitation of liability, each party's aggregate contractual liability arising out of or in connection with this DPA shall not exceed two times the fees paid or payable by Customer to Verifsage during the 12 months immediately preceding the event giving rise to liability.

15.4Each party ("Indemnifying Party") shall indemnify the other party against reasonable third-party claims, damages, liabilities and external legal costs to the extent directly caused by the Indemnifying Party's material breach of this DPA or Applicable Data Protection Law in connection with the Processing governed by this DPA.

15.5No indemnity applies to the extent the relevant loss was caused by the indemnified party's breach, negligence, unlawful instructions or failure to comply with Applicable Data Protection Law.

15.6Regulatory fines, penalties and sanctions shall be recoverable between the parties only to the extent such recovery is permitted by applicable law and only to the extent attributable to the Indemnifying Party's breach.

15.7Nothing in this clause alters any right of contribution, recourse or compensation established directly by Applicable Data Protection Law.

16. Confidentiality

16.1Information disclosed in connection with this DPA, including security documentation, audit evidence, incident information and details of technical systems, shall be treated as Confidential Information under the Agreement.

16.2Where the Agreement contains no confidentiality provisions, each party shall protect the other party's non-public information using at least reasonable care and shall use it only to perform or enforce the Agreement, this DPA or comply with applicable law.

17. Priority

17.1If there is a conflict concerning Processing of Customer Personal Data:

(a) the EU SCCs or UK Addendum prevail where applicable to the relevant Restricted Transfer;

(b) this DPA prevails over the Agreement to the extent of the data-protection conflict; and

(c) the remainder of the Agreement continues to apply.

18. Changes to law

18.1Verifsage may amend this DPA where reasonably necessary to reflect changes in Applicable Data Protection Law, regulatory guidance, transfer mechanisms or the Services, provided that no amendment materially reduces the protection afforded to Customer Personal Data contrary to Applicable Data Protection Law.

18.2Verifsage shall provide reasonable notice of any material amendment through its website, email or the Services.

18.3Where Applicable Data Protection Law requires an amendment to be signed or otherwise specifically accepted, the parties shall cooperate to implement that amendment.

19. Term and survival

19.1This DPA begins when Verifsage first Processes Customer Personal Data under the Agreement and continues for so long as Verifsage Processes Customer Personal Data.

19.2Clauses intended by their nature to continue after termination, including confidentiality, deletion, audit rights relating to pre-termination Processing, transfer obligations and liability provisions, shall survive termination for the period reasonably necessary to give them effect.

20.1Except where the EU SCCs, UK Addendum or mandatory Applicable Data Protection Law provides otherwise, this DPA shall be governed by the law governing the Agreement.

Schedule One

Details of Processing

Subject matter

Processing of Customer Personal Data as necessary to provide, operate, secure, maintain, administer and support the Services purchased or used by Customer.

Duration

For the term of the Agreement and such limited period thereafter as is necessary to return or securely delete Customer Personal Data, satisfy applicable retention requirements and complete backup deletion cycles.

Nature of Processing

Collection, receipt, access, organisation, structuring, hosting, storage, retrieval, consultation, transmission, use, analysis, support, troubleshooting, security monitoring, restriction, deletion and other Processing necessary to provide the Services in accordance with Customer's documented instructions.

Purpose of Processing

Provision, operation, maintenance, security and support of the Services; performance of Customer's documented instructions; prevention and investigation of security incidents; service administration; and compliance with legal obligations applicable to Verifsage as Processor.

Categories of Data Subjects

Various categories, which may include:

(a) Customer personnel and authorised users;

(b) Customer's customers;

(c) Customer's end-users;

(d) Customer employees, workers, contractors and representatives; and

(e) other individuals whose Personal Data Customer submits to or causes to be Processed through the Services.

Categories of Personal Data

Various categories of Personal Data submitted to, generated through or otherwise Processed using the Services, as determined by Customer and the functionality used.

These may include account, contact, identifier, transaction, device, technical, usage, authentication, communication, support and other Personal Data submitted by or on behalf of Customer.

Customer shall not knowingly submit special-category Personal Data, criminal-offence data or other highly sensitive Personal Data unless the relevant Services are expressly designed or agreed to Process such information and Customer has satisfied the applicable legal requirements.

Frequency

Continuous or intermittent, according to Customer's use of the Services.

Retention

For the duration described above, subject to Customer configuration, Verifsage retention schedules, backup cycles and applicable legal requirements.

Schedule Two

Technical and Organisational Measures

Verifsage shall maintain measures appropriate to the risk of the Processing, including the following to the extent applicable to the relevant systems and Services.

Access control and identity management. Access to Customer Personal Data shall be limited according to business need and least-privilege principles. Privileged or administrative access shall be restricted to authorised personnel and protected using appropriate authentication controls, including multi-factor authentication where technically supported and appropriate. Access rights shall be reviewed periodically and revoked when no longer required.

Encryption and transmission security. Customer Personal Data shall be protected in transit using appropriately configured industry-standard encrypted communication protocols. Customer Personal Data stored in production systems shall be protected through appropriate encryption or equivalent platform-level storage safeguards where supported and appropriate to the processing risk. Encryption keys and privileged secrets shall be subject to appropriate access restrictions.

Logical separation. Verifsage shall maintain technical or logical measures designed to prevent one customer from accessing another customer's Personal Data without authorisation.

Logging and monitoring. Relevant production and security systems shall generate logs appropriate to their function. Verifsage shall maintain monitoring designed to identify suspicious, anomalous or unauthorised activity and shall protect relevant security logs against inappropriate access or alteration.

Vulnerability and patch management. Verifsage shall maintain processes for identifying, assessing, prioritising and remediating material vulnerabilities in systems used to Process Customer Personal Data. Security patches shall be deployed according to risk, severity and operational constraints.

Secure development. Software development affecting systems that Process Customer Personal Data shall be subject to appropriate change control and security practices, including code review, dependency management, testing and separation of development and production environments where appropriate.

Malware and endpoint protection. Systems and endpoints used to administer or Process Customer Personal Data shall employ appropriate protections against malicious software and unauthorised access, taking account of system type and risk.

Availability and resilience. Verifsage shall maintain appropriate measures intended to support availability, resilience and recovery of systems used to provide the Services, including backup or equivalent recovery mechanisms where appropriate.

Backup and restoration. Where Customer Personal Data is backed up, Verifsage shall protect backups against unauthorised access and maintain processes for recovery or restoration appropriate to the Services.

Incident response. Verifsage shall maintain incident-management procedures covering identification, escalation, containment, investigation, recovery, evidence preservation and post-incident review.

Personnel security. Personnel with access to Customer Personal Data shall be subject to confidentiality obligations and appropriate privacy and security awareness measures. Access shall be revoked or adjusted upon termination or material change of role.

Supplier management. Verifsage shall conduct proportionate privacy and security due diligence before appointing material Subprocessors and shall contractually impose appropriate confidentiality, security and data-protection obligations.

Data minimisation and retention. Verifsage shall configure and operate the Services and its internal processes to avoid retaining Customer Personal Data longer than reasonably necessary for the authorised Processing, subject to Customer instructions and applicable legal obligations.

Deletion and disposal. Customer Personal Data and media containing it shall be securely deleted, destroyed or rendered inaccessible when no longer required, subject to backup lifecycle and legal retention requirements.

Physical security. Physical facilities controlled by Verifsage that host systems Processing Customer Personal Data shall be subject to appropriate access restrictions. Where infrastructure is hosted by third-party cloud or data-centre providers, Verifsage shall rely on the provider's physical security controls subject to appropriate supplier due diligence.

Testing and review. Verifsage shall periodically assess the effectiveness of relevant security controls and shall remediate material deficiencies according to risk.

Business continuity. Verifsage shall maintain proportionate business-continuity and disaster-recovery arrangements for material production services and shall periodically review their effectiveness.

Security governance. Responsibility for information security and privacy controls shall be allocated within Verifsage, with relevant policies, standards or procedures maintained and reviewed periodically.

Schedule Four

International Transfer Mechanisms

For EEA Customer Personal Data, transfers to the United Kingdom may presently rely on the European Commission's UK adequacy decision where the transfer falls within its scope. Other non-EEA transfers shall rely on an applicable adequacy decision or, where unavailable, the appropriate module of the EU SCCs and any necessary supplementary measures. The Commission renewed UK adequacy on 19 December 2025.

For UK Customer Personal Data, Verifsage shall first determine whether applicable UK adequacy regulations cover the destination. Where they do not, Verifsage may use the UK Addendum to the EU SCCs, the ICO's IDTA or another permitted Article 46 safeguard, together with any required transfer risk assessment and additional safeguards.

Where the EU SCCs apply, the selections in clause 11.3 govern unless the applicable Order Form expressly specifies different valid selections.

Where the UK Addendum applies, its mandatory clauses are incorporated by reference in the form approved by the ICO and in force at the relevant time. The ICO currently confirms that the existing Addendum remains available for use while amendments following the DUAA are being developed.

No provision of this DPA modifies an EU SCC, UK Addendum or other approved transfer instrument in a manner that reduces the protection afforded to Data Subjects or invalidates the relevant transfer safeguard.
